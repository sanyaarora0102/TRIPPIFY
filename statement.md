##  Problem statement

Planning a trip can sometimes be confusing, especially when people have a fixed budget but are not sure where to go, where to stay, or what to
**Trippify** is a simple Python-based travel planning project.

## Scope of the Project

The scope of Trippify is to provide a basic travel-planning experience
through a Python program.


## Target Users

Trippify is mainly designed for:

-   Students and young travellers
-   Families and groups
-   First-time travellers
-   Budget-conscious travellers

## High-Level Features

The main features of Trippify include:

1.  User Details
2.  Budget-Based Destination Selection
3.  Mountain or Beach Selection
4.  Destination Recommendations
5.  Hotel Suggestions
6.  Places to Visit
7.  Packing Checklist
8.  Interactive User Experience
